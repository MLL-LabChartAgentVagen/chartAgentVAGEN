The Latent Data World

Evaluating VLM agents on charts as projections of a hidden table.

Pingyue Zhang · Northwestern University · chartAgent / VAGEN

A delivery dashboard reports 31 → 18 min.

SwiftEats rolls out a new dispatcher. Average delivery time falls in one week.

Headline metric
Average pickup-to-drop time: 31 min (old) → 18 min (new).
Same week, same dashboard
Refunds, cancellations, and timeouts all rise.
An agent must reconcile both facts from the chart alone.

Four candidate explanations for the 31 → 18 gap.

Each is testable from charts derived from the same underlying table.

h₁
Genuine speedup

The new dispatcher shortens routes across every order, every zone, every time of day.

h₂
Zone selection

The platform stops accepting orders in distant or low-density zones. Only easy orders remain.

h₃
Denominator change

The 18-minute figure averages only successful deliveries. Cancellations and timeouts are excluded.

h₄
Metric boundary

The 18 minutes covers pickup-to-drop only. Click-to-pickup waiting falls outside the window.

Phase 2 — the LLM writes Python, not JSON.

Two ordered steps. Eight call types. Every column declares its own data-generating program.

Step 1 · column declarations
add_category  categorical, group, parent
add_temporal  time grain, derived calendar
add_measure  stochastic root, y ~ Dist(θ)
add_measure_structural  DAG-derived, closed form
Step 2 · relationships and patterns
declare_orthogonal  independence across groups
add_group_dependency  root-only cross-group DAG
inject_pattern  trend, outlier, ranking reversal
set_realism  noise, missingness, censoring
Each measure is declared once. No patching across calls.

SwiftEats — zone × algorithm drives both outcomes.

Acceptance and delivery time both depend on the interaction of zone and algorithm.

# Step 1: columns
sim.add_category("zone_tier", values=["downtown","mid","peripheral"],
                  weights=[0.45,0.35,0.20], group="geo")
sim.add_category("algorithm", values=["old","new"], weights=[0.5,0.5], group="system")
sim.add_measure("distance_km", family="lognormal",
                 param_model={"mu":1.5, "sigma":0.6})

# Step 2: relationships
sim.add_group_dependency("accepted", on=["zone_tier","algorithm"],
    conditional_weights={
       ("peripheral","new"): {"yes":0.41, "no":0.59},
       ("peripheral","old"): {"yes":0.82, "no":0.18}, # …
    })
sim.add_measure_structural("pickup_to_dropoff_min",
    formula="8 + 3.5*distance_km - 4*(algorithm=='new')",
    noise={"family":"gaussian", "sigma":2.0})
sim.inject_pattern("dominance_shift", target="algorithm=='new' & zone_tier=='peripheral'",
    col="accepted", params={"drop_rate":0.43})
Every behavior the agent must discover is declared once in the script.

Validation runs at the SDK level, before any chart.

Typed exceptions feed back to the LLM. Three validator layers run after every build. Up to three retries.

Attempt 1 · unbounded speedup
sim.add_measure_structural("pickup_to_dropoff_min",
   formula="5 - 30*(algorithm=='new')")
Validator L2: min(pickup→drop) = −25 < 0 ⇒ SDKException
Attempt 2 · bounded, distance-aware
sim.add_measure_structural("pickup_to_dropoff_min",
   formula="8 + 3.5*distance_km - 4*(algorithm=='new')",
   noise={"sigma":2.0})
All three layers pass. 1000 atomic rows written.
Three validator layers
L1 · Structural
Schema valid. Types compatible. Every DAG acyclic.
L2 · Statistical
Moments in range. Correlations feasible. Bounds respected.
L3 · Pattern
Injected trends, drop-outs, and outliers are recoverable.

C2 · Active Chart Reasoning.

Four moves. Two costly actions. One bbox-grounded answer.

Four moves: Read, Reveal, Reconcile, Debunk.

Each move names a specific question the agent must answer about the chart.

1
Read — what does the metric mean?

Identify the two timestamps the metric spans. Identify which orders are in the denominator.

2
Reveal — what dimension is missing?

Stratify by zone tier. Check whether the new algorithm stopped accepting peripheral orders.

3
Reconcile — how can both charts be true?

Delivery time fell and refunds rose. Find the funnel structure under which both can hold.

4
Debunk — does the metric survive a fair yardstick?

Widen the window to click-to-door. Include every cancelled and timed-out order. Recompute.

Five actions. Two cost budget.
